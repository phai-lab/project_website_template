Abstract

This page is a live example of the PHAI Lab project-page template. Every section you see here — the author list, link buttons, teaser, video, method figures, results grid, side-by-side comparison, results table, team photos, and BibTeX — is a drop-in block you can copy, fill in, or delete. To create a new paper page, copy template.html, replace the placeholders, and you are done. Re-theming the accent color takes a single line in assets/css/phai.css.
