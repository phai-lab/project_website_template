Abstract

This page is a live example of the PHAI Lab project-page template. Its structure mirrors well-developed robotics project pages — a labeled teaser, an abstract, a paper block with a clickable thumbnail, an icon grid for code and data, a team section with photos, and BibTeX — followed by your method, results, and video. Every block is drop-in: copy template.html, replace the placeholders, and you are done. Re-theming the accent color takes a single line in assets/css/phai.css.
